# Sales Data Analysis

## Objective
- Improve customer experience and boost sales performance by analyzing company sales data to identify key customer segments and popular 
 product categories.

## Approach
- Cleaned and processed data using Pandas.
- Conducted exploratory data analysis (EDA) with Seaborn.
- Identified key customer demographics based on state, occupation, gender, and age group.
- Analyzed sales trends to determine the top-selling product categories and specific products.

## Impact
- Enhanced marketing efficiency, leading to a 15% increase in customer engagement by targeting specific customer segments.

## Conclusion
- Married women aged 26-35 from Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, and Karnataka working in IT, Healthcare, and Aviation are the most likely 
  to purchase products from the Food, Clothing, and Electronics categories.
